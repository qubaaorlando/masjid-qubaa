OUR CREED & DA’WAH

Who Are We?

We are Muslims who call to Tawheed (the Oneness of Allah) and adhere to the Qur’an and authentic Sunnah according to the understanding of the Salaf (the righteous early generations of Islam).

We believe in Allah and affirm His Names and Attributes as mentioned in the Qur’an and Sunnah, without distortion, denial, or likening them to creation.

  • We call to Tawheed and warn against shirk, including calling upon the dead and seeking from creation what only Allah can provide.
  • We adhere to the Qur’an and authentic Sunnah, relying upon sound evidence in matters of belief, worship, and religious rulings.
  • We follow the understanding of the Salaf, referring to the righteous early generations of this Ummah, particularly the first three generations of Muslims.
  • We love the Companions of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and his family, and we defend their honor in accordance with the teachings of Islam.
  • We love Ahlus-Sunnah and the scholars of the Sunnah, benefit from their knowledge, and respect their contributions to preserving the Religion.
  • We reject fabricated narrations and baseless religious claims and strive to ensure that what we teach is supported by authentic evidence.
  • We believe that the Qur’an is the Speech of Allah and is not created. We also affirm the authentic teachings concerning the Hereafter, including the believers seeing their Lord and the intercession.
  • We oppose sectarianism and divisive partisanship and call Muslims to cooperate upon truth and righteousness.
  • We believe that Islam encompasses all aspects of life, including public affairs, and that the honor and victory of the Muslims lie in returning to the Qur’an and Sunnah.
  • We oppose rebellion against Muslim rulers and emphasize avoiding the bloodshed, corruption, and division that revolutions can cause, while adhering to the principles and conditions outlined by the scholars.
  • We accept sincere advice and correction, acknowledge that we can be right or wrong, and strive to follow the truth rather than blindly adhering to individuals.
  • We love the Sunnah and encourage learning and practicing it, rejecting the claim that its teachings are no longer relevant to our times.
  • We remain firm upon our creed and da’wah, refusing to compromise our religious principles for wealth, status, or worldly gain.

Our call is founded upon the Qur’an and authentic Sunnah, understood according to the way of the Salaf. We strive to adhere to the truth, call to Allah with knowledge, and remain steadfast upon our religion until we meet Him.

Adapted from the statement of Shaykh Muqbil bin Hadi al-Wadi‘i, “This Is Our Aqeedah and Our Da’wah.”
